Question 9

Responsive by principle

A short summary of the habits behind adaptable interfaces.
01

Start with mobile

Design the smallest useful experience first, then enhance it for wider screens.

02

Use fluid systems

Prefer flexible grids, percentages, minmax(), and clamp() over fixed coordinates.

03

Respect content

Let text wrap and images scale instead of forcing people to zoom or scroll sideways.

04

Test real sizes

Check touch targets, contrast, and layout at several widths before publishing.
